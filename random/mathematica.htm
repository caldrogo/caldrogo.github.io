Running decomposeTensorProd[ ThreeOne\[TensorProduct]ThreeOne\[TensorProduct]ThreeOne], decomposes the tensor product of ThreeOne and ThreeOne into the other irreducible representations.

Running decomposeTensorProd[ ThreeOne\[TensorProduct]ThreeOne\[TensorProduct]ThreeOne], which is four copies of the representation ThreeOne gives,
